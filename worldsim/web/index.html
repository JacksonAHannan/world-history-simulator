About this model

This is an exploratory worldbuilding tool. It generates fictional histories from geography, food, human-like cohorts, social institutions and contact.

History is conditional. There are no dated technology unlocks, predetermined empires or inherited advantages for communities. Political control, language, identity and belief are separate.

Population and stocks are accounted for every year. Every history entry links to a recorded event and its conditions. Climate, fiscal values, conflict and language similarity use coarse model rules.

External historical calibration and expert review have not been performed. The atlas reports engine counts and explicitly labeled estimates.
